# C9. $c$ indexes a family of invariant forms, and $c \to \infty$ is where the form degenerates

*Approved at Gate 3 as a supporting mathematical connection. Claude proposed it.*

## Established mathematics (Level 1, carded)

Write spacetime's form on $(t, x)$ with $c$ as a parameter:

$$M_c = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1/c^2 \end{pmatrix}.$$

1. Every Lorentz boost $\Lambda_c$ preserves $M_c$: $\Lambda_c^T M_c \Lambda_c = M_c$. *(C9a; S5c)*
2. As $c \to \infty$, $M_c \to \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$. The form becomes degenerate: absolute time emerges in the Galilean limit. *(C9b)*
3. The boost generator $K_c = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -1/c^2 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ has $\det K_c = -1/c^2 < 0$, so it is hyperbolic. Its limit $K_\infty$ has $K_\infty^2 = 0$, which is the **shear**, the middle case of S4's trichotomy. *(C9c)*
4. $\exp(vK_\infty) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -v & 1 \end{pmatrix}$ is the Galilean boost, and it preserves the degenerate form. *(C9d; S5d)*

So the Lorentz boost representation contracts to a Galilean shear. This is a bridge between:

- the invariant form;
- the transformation group;
- $c$ as a parameter;
- a boundary between theories.

## What C9 does *not* say

$$\boxed{\text{spacetime geometry} \neq \text{model-manifold geometry}.}$$

- Points 1–4 concern **spacetime's** form. The paper's statement concerns the **model manifold**: classical kinematics as the $c \to \infty$ boundary of the manifold of special relativities ([source](../sources/quinn2023.md)).
- The two are linked **only because the same parameter $c$** indexes both:
  - the family of spacetime structures;
  - the path through theory/prediction space.
- C9 does not prove they are one manifold.
- **C9 is not inferred from the paper.** The paper supports the model-manifold boundary statement. C9 is an additional mathematical connection.
