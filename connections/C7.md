## Where it might break (Claude's notes, not carded)

- **Different objects.** Bessel's inequality bounds the coefficients of *one fixed function* in a basis. A sloppy spectrum is the eigenvalues of $J^T J$, the *metric*. For "the same pattern" to mean something, the analogy has to say what plays $f$ and what plays $e_k$. One candidate: the Fisher eigenvectors as the basis, and the prediction differences as the function. That pairing is Claude's guess and needs your call.
