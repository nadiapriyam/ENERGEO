# Author philosophy (provenance)

This is a holding document. It records the intellectual origin of the project in Nadia's words, and it is not
learner-facing. A future "About the Author / Why Zero?" section may draw on it, but this file is never polished
into copy.

The words are Nadia's (backbone clarification, 2026-11-11), quoted exactly. LaTeX boxes were converted to plain
text. Each section names the registry record that keeps the idea, and the readings Nadia said must not be imposed
on it yet. Nothing here is presented as a mathematical result. For internal construction these ideas are
foundational targets (`docs/research_method.md`).

---

## 1. Existing things as zeros

> People, and more generally things that exist, can be thought of analogously as zeros: what appears as one formed thing may be the cancellation, balance, or organization of many things underneath it.

> Can an apparently singular existing object be represented as the stable zero/balance of a richer set of relations?

> Preserve the theory before explaining it.

Not to be equated with the Stage 3 mass example. The idea is related in spirit, but it is not yet the same
mathematical object; Nadia: "Do not conclude: therefore existence = center of mass." Registry: `T-EXISTENCE-ZERO`.

## 2. What are we to the fractal?

> People are also part of the fractal. We are not simply “in” a fractal as external observers. We are in one as constituents of it.

> The question is not “Where on the fractal are we?” The question is “What are we to the fractal?”

> part ↔ whole geometry

> What mathematical properties would the project's geometry need in order for Nadia's part/whole “we are to the fractal” intuition to become exact?

The word *fractal* stays. Not yet to be translated into fractal dimension, Mandelbrot coordinates, scale invariance,
self-similarity, renormalization or cosmology: these may later become tests, but they are not the statement.
Registry: `B-PART-WHOLE`, `T-FRACTAL-PROPERTIES-Q`.

## 3. Exploring space

> Humans treat space exploration as exploring something outside our world. But if we are constituents of the same larger structure, exploring outward remains an exploration of the structure of which we are already a part.

> If we are part of the fractal, it is impossible to explore the fractal without continually encountering the structure that also produced us.

Not a cosmological claim. It may later generate mathematical questions about observer/object relationships.
Registry: `T-SPACE-EXPLORATION`.

## 4. Looking inside oneself, looking inside zero

> A Buddhist-style idea says that looking inside oneself holds the key. In this project, looking inside zero plays an analogous role.

> Do not destroy the zero to understand it. Look inside its structure.

Not a mathematical proof, and not deleted because it is philosophical. The second sentence is also the project's
working sentence for the zero program (B-ZERO-PRESERVE). Registry: `T-LOOK-INSIDE`.

## 5. Finite shape, infinite structure

> An infinite structure can be bound/generated by rules that manifest as a finite shape.

> What kinds of finite geometries can encode or generate indefinitely continuing structure through their internal rules?

> What makes a geometric form stable enough that its rules persist while the structure continues indefinitely?

The circle, (cos θ, sin θ) with θ ∈ ℝ, is one known case. The theory is not reduced to it. Registry:
`B-FINITE-INFINITE`, `T-FINITE-GEOMETRIES-Q`, `T-STABLE-FORM-Q`.

## 6. Existence and irreversible geometric formation

> Our existence may only be possible because geometry produces something that becomes irreversibly formed, while still belonging to an endlessly continuing/fractal structure.

> irreversible geometric formation

Definition OPEN. These are candidate meanings to investigate later, and none is selected now: thermodynamic
irreversibility, entropy production, topological protection, symmetry breaking, measurement collapse, hysteresis.
These are known examples, not an exhaustive classification for this project. Registry: `T-IRREVERSIBLE-FORMATION`.

## 7. Seed: "0 is orthogonal"

> 0 is orthogonal.

Kept as a foundational theory seed, not replaced with a textbook correction. Its four readings (A–D) are separate
construction branches, and none is chosen as the intended one. Registry: `T-ZERO-ORTHOGONAL` and
`T-ZERO-ORTHOGONAL-A` … `-D`.
